Manufacturing project details

Antigravity table

Our team designed and manufactured an antigravity table, exploring the principles of balance and counterweight systems. The table combined woodworking and welding techniques to create a functional and visually striking piece. Through this project, I gained hands-on experience in workshop processes while applying engineering concepts to a practical design.

My contributions included:

  • Conceptualizing and designing the antigravity table
  • Fabricating components using woodworking and welding techniques
  • Overseeing assembly and ensuring mechanical balance and stability
