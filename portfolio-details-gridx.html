GridX Challenge details

GridX Steam Challenge

As part of a national initiative, We participated in the inaugural GridX STEAM Motorsport Challenge, Sri Lanka's first-ever event combining Science, Technology, Engineering, Arts, and Mathematics in motorsports. While serving as team captain, our team Screwderia Carbon, designed, built, and raced a CO2-powered miniature car, achieving 1st Runner-Up in the Fastest Car category.

  • Collaborated in a multidisciplinary team to design a high-performance CO2-powered car
  • Modeled the car body and components using Fusion 360 with a focus on weight reduction and structural stability
  • Conducted aerodynamic simulations using ANSYS Fluent to improve airflow and minimize drag
  • Contributed to the fabrication and testing phases, ensuring alignment with simulation results
  • Evaluated performance metrics and refined design for improved launch stability and acceleration

This project enhanced my expertise in CAD modeling, CFD simulation, teamwork, and design optimization — blending engineering creativity with real-world competition experience.
